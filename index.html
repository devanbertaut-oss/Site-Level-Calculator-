What's New

Site Level Calculator v1.04 — a field-accuracy and data-safety update:

  • Offsets and stations are no longer capped — offsets past 99 ft and stations past 999+99 used to be silently rescaled as you typed (125 became 12.50). They are entered as typed now.
  • Shots record their whole derivation — benchmark, BM elevation, backsight, HI and foresight are stored and exported next to the answer, so a run can be re-checked in the office.
  • Turn points — a new ↺ Turn button carries the shot you just took forward as the next setup's benchmark, and every shot is stamped with its setup number.
  • Nothing important deletes without asking — Clear All and the per-shot ✕ now confirm, and the ✕ is a full-size target instead of a thumbnail.
  • It tells you when it can't save — if phone storage is blocked or full, a red bar says so instead of the app quietly dropping every shot.
  • Works fully offline — the map library and fonts ship with the app now, and a weak signal can no longer leave you on a blank screen.
  • Sunlight mode reads properly — the gold text, the active tab and the GPS button were all below contrast standards in bright light, and the whole small-text layer is now larger in that mode.
  • Pinch-zoom works — zoom was disabled app-wide.

Always verify against control and double-check critical shots.
