Site Level Calculator

📱 How to put it on your phone
Step 1 — Open this in your phone's browser
watershedsitelevel.netlify.app
iPhone iOS use Safari
1
Tap the "..." button
Bottom right of Safari — the three dots in a row.
2
Tap "Share"
A menu pops up — the Share button is the square with an up-arrow.
3
Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen"
It's in the list of actions below the row of app icons.
4
Tap "Add" in the top right
The icon will be ready to use on your home screen.
Android Phone use Chrome
1
Tap the three dots
Top right corner of Chrome, next to the address bar.
2
Tap "Add to Home screen"
May also say "Install app" — either one works.
3
Tap "Add" or "Install"
The icon will be ready to use on your home screen.
✓ You're done
Tap the new icon any time to open Site Level Calculator. Works full-screen like a real app, and keeps working even with no signal once it's loaded the first time.
HEADS UP — On iPhone, you have to use Safari for the Share button to show "Add to Home Screen." Chrome on iPhone won't have it. On Android, the exact wording may say Install app or Add to Home screen depending on the phone — either one works. First time you open it, give it a few seconds with signal so the app saves itself for offline use.
